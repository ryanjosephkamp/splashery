Find your own splat

Where to find one

SuperSplat shows many scenes, and some can be downloaded. Check each one's licence: the toys on this shelf only use CC0, CC BY or public domain work (see Creative Commons licences). You can open any file you own or may use; it stays on your device.

Make your own

Scan something with a phone app, then export it as a splat: Scaniverse (free), Polycam, Luma or KIRI Engine. Walk slowly all the way round in soft, even light.

Formats

.ply, .splat, .spz and .sog. First crop away stray splats in the SuperSplat editor, so the toy sits in the middle and nothing floats around it.

What works

Poke, Paint and Magnet, every effect, motion, and flag and pattern colours. The Detail setting and the automatic resolution apply too.

What doesn't

Actions made for one toy (opening a lid, blowing out a candle) and Clay. A file can't go in a share link, because it never leaves your device; export a PNG, GIF or video instead.

Controls

  • Drag turns the toy; with a tool picked, drag on the toy uses it
  • Right-drag, Space + drag or two fingers always orbit
  • Wheel or pinch zooms; two-finger twist rolls
  • Double-click or double-tap resets the view
  • 1–5 pick tools, P pokes, R resets, Arrows and + / − move the camera
