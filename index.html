Controls & keys
  • Drag paints in Paint mode, orbits in Orbit mode
  • Space + drag, or two fingers, orbits while painting
  • Wheel or pinch zooms; no limit
  • Double-click in Orbit mode resets the camera
  • P / O switch modes, Z undoes, R resets the camera
  • [ and ] change brush size
  • Arrows, +, − turn and zoom from the keyboard
