# The hands-on plan

Drafted October 2, 2026, by lane Physics, for the owner's approval.

With the ✋ Hands-on switch on, **Level 1** works for every toy, scans included: you pick the whole
toy up and toss it, and it lands, bounces and settles (soft toys squish). ↺ Reset sends it home.
Each line below says what a toy does **beyond Level 1**: the pieces you can grab (a kit recipe's
tokens, up to 48, or its rigid parts, up to 15), the joints they swing on, or the soft parts you
stretch. "Level 1 only." means the toy stays as it is. The owner marks each line approve, change or
skip on a review page the Operator makes; category lanes then build the approved lines, each held to
the effect quality rules in CLAUDE.md. "Built by lane Physics." marks the showcase toys this lane is
building now, and "Already hands-on" marks toys that keep their own controls.
