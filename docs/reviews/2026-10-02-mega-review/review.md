# The owner's review of October 2, 2026 (the mega review)

His notes file `splashery-mega-review.md`, sent to the Operator early on October 2, 2026 (UTC; the
evening of October 1 in Eastern time), word for word. He went through every toy on the preview build
(main plus every open lane branch, `claude/operator-preview-oct1`), sounds and looks together. He
did not review the Worlds this time and asked to pause them.

With it he sent five pictures. Three are screenshots of the site, kept here:

- `burger-bun.jpg`: the burger's top bun, which flashes white as you zoom in.
- `splat-equation-seam.jpg`: the seam line on the splat equation's torus (the Möbius strip shows one
  too).
- `thermal-ellipsoids.jpg`: the thermal ellipsoids toy's aspirin as solid ellipsoids, far too big.

The other two were photos of Theodore Gray's periodic table poster and book cover _The Elements_,
sent as a style reference for a photoreal elements table. They are copyrighted and are not kept in
the repository.

The Operator's itemized list (every note by toy, with the lane that takes it) and its answers are on
the private alignment page; the sound notes are in `tools/sound-review.json` (round "2026-10-02").
