The Operator's reading: the black boxes are a rendering bug, not a rule (nothing in Splashery hides
anything). Lane Viewer (PR #75) fixes them, makes pinch zoom only, adds the tilt lock, Reset view,
the Turntable and Tilt lock buttons, flags per toy under Look, and the terms of use. The tilt lock
follows each toy (flat toys start locked) rather than being one global switch; the owner may ask for
a global one.
