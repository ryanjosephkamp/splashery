## Brief

Written by the Operator from the old HANDOFF.md ("Phase E5 in detail: food", 2026-09-26).

Before starting, do a lane's start (OPERATING.md): check the owner's marks on the Toy Plan page for
your toys for any later change. Follow the Effect quality rules in CLAUDE.md, the draw-order lessons
and the channel kinds in docs/PACKS.md (6 and 7b), and post clips of every new effect on the Effect
review page (OPERATING.md, "Steps for a lane").

1. **Toys (17 new).** Watermelon, cupcake, lollipop, candy cane, macarons, pretzel, croissant,
   sushi, taco, apple, bananas, orange, kiwi, pineapple, cherries, grapes, avocado. Build each
   planned effect (TOY-PLAN.md, wave E5) as `controls`, `action` and `drive()` in its recipe
   (`src/packs/food.js`). The 10 food toys already marked keep (ice cream, birthday cake, popcorn,
   jelly, pancakes, gummy bear, pizza, burger, boiled egg, coffee) stay as they are.
2. **Twins.** The croissant must act and sound different from the real croissant scan, and the
   grapes from the grape scan (whose peel is the bar).
3. **Food rules.** Things that are cut, peeled or bitten come apart as real pieces with their
   insides showing (built as cut faces, like the E1 croissant and pomegranate add-ons), and go back
   together or regrow. Separate things move separately (each cherry, each grape, each macaron).
4. **Sounds.** Re-time each toy's sound to its new effect with `tools/sound-check.mjs` (under five
   seconds; `out.cues` for later sounds). Only the Operator republishes the Sound Board; if the
   owner wants to hear yours before the merge, publish your own copy (OPERATING.md, "Generated
   files").
5. Check each effect with `tools/effect-clip.mjs --strip=8`, look at a larger still of its fullest
   moment, re-render thumbnails only where the resting look changes, mark each finished toy
   `"v": "keep"` with an `improved` entry, and regenerate TOY-PLAN.md. `tests/taps.spec.mjs` then
   checks each finished toy's tap by itself (it ends where the toy rests); put any extra tests in
   `tests/e5.spec.mjs`.

**Done when:** every E5 toy has its own tap effect (or the PR lists which do not, and why), all
tests pass and prettier is clean.

## Notes

- Twins: the croissant opens like a roll (the real croissant scan tears in half) and the bunch of
  grapes drops grapes (the grape scan peels). `tests/e5.spec.mjs` checks their actions and sounds
  differ.
- Cut pieces are tokens with their cut faces built in (orange wedges, watermelon slices, pineapple
  rings as parts, taco halves as parts); the faces sit inside the fruit at rest, so they only show
  once the pieces move.
- Built open, hidden when closed: the kiwi's halves are built where they are seen open (so they draw
  in the right order) and turned back at rest; their faces are pieces of their own, hidden while the
  halves are nearly closed; their skin is lit as it lies at rest (the normal turned back) so the two
  halves match.
- Two copies for the draw order: the avocado's stone is built twice, once in each half, and the two
  swap while it flies fast; the watermelon's knife is built once per cut.
- The apple is a toggle with a two-part story: its drive() remembers which way the toggle is going
  (mem), so the bite plays on the way up and the worm and the healing on the way down.
- The pretzel's contortion is one morph: each splat keeps its place across the rope (a frame at its
  point along the pretzel, carried to the same point along the contorted knot); the channel runs
  negative for the opposite contortion as it springs back. (Its first version, undoing itself into a
  U of rope, was marked "Needs work": the owner wants knot-like twisting, not undoing.)
- The croissant's first version (rising in an oven glow and shedding flakes) was marked "Needs
  work"; it now opens along its middle like a roll (the top is a part that lifts and tips back),
  with cut faces showing the layers and a pat of butter that melts on a morph channel.
- The bananas' first version peeled only the front one; the owner wants all three to separate and
  each to peel. Each banana is now seven pieces (body and six peel pieces), all moved by the
  banana's own separation, 21 tokens in all.
- Later sounds are cues from drive() (`cuesAt` in food.js): chops, landings, snaps, the butter's
  sizzle.

## Known issues

- The default style of each toy has the full effect. Some other styles do less: a lone watermelon
  wedge and a lone kiwi half only rock, and a lone orange half gives a small squeeze.
- The avocado does not "split open" (it is already two halves); its stone hops between the halves
  instead.
- Small draw-order slips remain where pieces cross: a taco half's edge over the other half as they
  swing, the dipped sushi roll a little in front of the dish's rim.
- The apple's bitten surface is a little mottled close up.
- The cupcake's sprinkles jump as fine coloured dots (about a third of them jump).

## For the Operator

- PACKS.md lessons: "built open, hidden when closed" (the kiwi) with lighting from the rest pose;
  "two copies swap while fast" (the avocado stone); toggles with a two-part story (the apple's
  direction memory); a morph that carries a tube's cross-section to a new centre line (the pretzel).
- Backlog (engine): `k.roundedBox` with a high power (7) leaves a thin band with no splats across
  its middle (the sushi board on main showed a dark stripe). E5 switched its board and tray to
  `k.box`; other toys using high-power rounded boxes may have it too.
- Test note: in one full run (2026-09-26) the smoke test "rigs pick splats by colour…" failed its
  strawberry check (the seeds had not settled 3.6 s after the tap: 66,287 pixels differed, the limit
  was 21,229); it passed when run again by itself. E5 does not touch the strawberry or its rig; it
  looks timing-sensitive under load, like the flaky drag in HANDOFF.md.
- The `chop` voice is a helicopter rotor, not a knife; knife chops here are `slap` plus `crack`. A
  voice note in `src/voices.js` or PACKS.md might save the next lane the surprise.
