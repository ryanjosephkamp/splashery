# Photoreal captures and Studio media sources

Checked October 5, 2026 (UTC). Prepared by dotio directly, without Codex or Work delegation.

## Summary

1. This report contains 45 distinct media entries across the seven requested areas.
2. It uses the push notes and the current Landmarks shelf, not just generic asset catalogs.
3. Real car and spacecraft scans exist, but a complete, mechanically faithful X-ray car remains a
   gap.
4. The Apollo interior has separate real surfaces; the car scan explicitly has missing interior
   parts.
5. Eight element samples plus a mercury-vial view can support a real-sample periodic table.
6. Six longer video originals meet 30 seconds–3 minutes and at least 720p; usable-shot checks still
   matter.
7. Five moving-photo inputs include a real historical GIF, and three stills offer different depth
   cues.
8. Four small textured GLBs were parsed and verified to be under 10 MB and free of Draco.
9. Ardvreck is NC; Winged Victory is NC and SA. Several photographs and videos are SA.
10. A license pass is not a release pass: remove marks/faces, honor credits, and test the actual
    conversion.

## What I read and what the labels mean

I read [the owner's push notes](../reviews/2026-10-04-push-alignment/notes.md),
[the earlier source audit](new-sources-2026-10.md), the new science-data report delivered in
[draft PR 279](https://github.com/ryanjosephkamp/splashery/pull/279), the Ground rules in
[CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md), and the `landmarks` entries in [src/toys.js](../../src/toys.js). This
report adds media, not another listing of the earlier science datasets. Nothing here changes a toy
or adds an asset to the shipped site.

**D / C** means derivatives / commercial use under the cited copyright license. BY requires credit,
a license link, and notice of changes. **SA** requires the adapted asset to keep the source license.
**NC** prohibits commercial use and requires `nc: true` in the asset registry. NC derivatives retain
the license under the project rules. Never merge an NC source and a BY-SA source into one asset. A
model of ancient sculpture is identified as sculpture, not a clinical anatomy source.

Every quoted license page or publisher license API field below was opened live on the checked date.
Short strings such as `cc-by-4.0` are the exact structured license identifiers, not invented prose.
For archived Sketchfab files, I also inspected embedded author/source/license fields where
available. The archive license is per asset; neither a hosting site's general text license nor a
software repository's code license clears unrelated media. No Pexels, Pixabay, ND,
personal-use-only, paid, or unlicensed item is in the 45-entry list.

All direct links below are published public-file routes with no account or key specified for those
routes. Some transfers timed out or were rate-limited; those are stated, not presented as successful
downloads. Large video originals were checked from their live file pages rather than fully
downloaded. Sizes are exact bytes when measured or explicitly reported; otherwise the publisher's
rounded size is labeled. Image resolution means the chosen file, not a different larger variant.
Model resolution means triangle/face count and texture dimensions when available, not a claim of
capture accuracy. **10 MB here means 10,000,000 bytes.**

The source names and creator names are necessary provenance in this report. They are not proposed
brand names for the toy interface. The lane must remove visible logos, maker badges, brand
lettering, and plate identifiers from derivatives before shipping. Credits must still name the
actual creator. Videos with people or marks are conditional candidates until those are removed
throughout the clip. I did not establish model releases or other publicity rights, and do not
recommend recognizable people's faces as samples. Copyright licensing does not waive privacy,
trademark, site-access, or other separate rights.

## Landmarks shelf match and honest gaps

The shelf currently has Eiffel Tower, Washington Monument, Pyramids of Giza, Twisting Supertall,
Lighthouse, Statue of Liberty, White House, Leaning Tower of Pisa, Colosseum, Parthenon, Stonehenge,
Big Ben, Taj Mahal, Castle, Pagoda, and Windmill.

- **Castle:** Lichtenstein and Ardvreck are documented real captures; Czorsztyn is a wall fragment.
- **Lighthouse:** Reykjanes is a licensed textured model of a real site, but its capture method is
  not documented. Do not silently promote that uncertainty into a photogrammetry claim.
- **Parthenon:** the frieze scan captures original architectural sculpture, not the temple building.
- **Historical sculpture:** Winged Victory is a real scan, but untextured, Draco-compressed, and
  NC/SA. It is a geometry candidate, not a finished photoreal color toy.
- **Remaining named shelf items:** this audit does not establish a directly downloadable, clean,
  fully licensed real capture for each one. A handmade model, a scan of a souvenir, or a model
  scraped from a proprietary map is not an equivalent substitute. Several tempting Colosseum and
  Eiffel results were exactly those kinds of mismatches.

A new
[Colosseum drone capture by eyesCloud3D](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/romes-colosseum-4577eb4b21ac48ecba43e7944b4e1baf)
was a promising search lead, but I did not verify its downloadable package, size, or complete rights
record. It is **not** one of the 45 cleared entries. The owner can pursue it later without confusing
it with the verified archive files above.

## Best first choices

1. **A real interior that peels apart:** start with the Apollo hull, seats, and panel. Decode Draco
   offline, preserve the original transforms, and show only the surfaces actually scanned.
2. **A real building:** start with the Lichtenstein capture. It is a documented real scan with a
   small, textured, no-Draco download, although its ground pedestal needs a deliberate presentation.
3. **Real element cards:** use the eight Images of Elements photos, with a visible link to each
   exact element page. Add the SA mercury-vial photo only with its own asset license and credit.
4. **Longer camera motion:** try a clean shot from Hardegg or Campell before a busy street. Check
   overlap, blur, cuts, repeated textures, and moving foliage rather than assuming duration means
   successful reconstruction.
5. **A moving-photo showcase:** try jellyfish and a waterfall, beside the unchanged original at
   matching timestamps. Explain that this is frame-by-frame inferred depth, not a recovered 4D scan.
6. **A reliable Studio format test:** use the avocado or lantern GLB first. Their authored surfaces
   are useful import tests, but should never be promoted as real photogrammetric captures.
7. **A data-grounded Moon:** combine the selected color and displacement maps. Keep the specified
   radius, units, and longitude convention; any vertical exaggeration needs an explicit label.

## Rejected shortcuts and unresolved technical work

- **The X-ray car is not solved.** The real compact-car scan has missing cabin pieces. Neither an
  outside surface capture nor a toy-car model proves what the engine, wiring, fuel system, or
  chassis looks like inside. A mechanically faithful layer-by-layer car needs a separately licensed
  complete engineering model or independently captured parts. The aircraft and capsule are better
  near-term peel-apart examples, with their own stated limits.
- **Do not infer no-Draco from a filename.** The Apollo `high.glb` files actually contain Draco. The
  four dedicated Studio samples were checked by parsing their GLB JSON and embedded images. The
  Saturn V has no Draco but uses WebP textures; Voyager has Draco and no image texture. Only the
  dedicated Studio table is promised to meet all three size/texture/no-Draco conditions as
  downloaded.
- **Real geometry is not always real color.** Winged Victory has no color texture. The Moon height
  TIFF stores elevation, and galaxy photos store projected light. Do not turn their display
  treatments into claims of measured hidden surfaces, complete spacecraft internals, or 3D galaxy
  distances.
- **Tanks and Temples is withheld.** Its
  [live license page](https://www.tanksandtemples.org/license/) says CC BY 4.0 in one section but
  later prohibits commercial use and redistribution without permission. I did not choose the
  permissive paragraph and ignore the conflict.
- **The familiar neon-tube image is rejected.**
  [This Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Neon_discharge_tube.jpg) offers GFDL
  1.2 and a BY-NC-ND alternative, neither acceptable here. The selected Images of Elements neon
  photograph has a separate, checked BY 3.0 grant.
- **The familiar rubber-duck GLB is rejected.** Its current
  [asset README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/main/Models/Duck/README.md)
  names the SCEA Shared Source License, not a license on the asset allowlist. The glTF collection's
  software status does not change that.
- **Do not use anonymous YouTube-derived scans without provenance.** Several castle models were
  described as reconstructed from a YouTube video without a verified upstream video license. They
  were not substituted for the documented captures.
- **No dancer sample was cleared.** The selected motion examples use animals, water, and historical
  animal photographs instead. Recognizable performers would need a separate rights/content review.
- **NASA marks are separate rights.** The official model repository says its assets are without
  copyright, but its linked [media rules](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
  reserve agency identifiers and prohibit implied endorsement. Remove insignia from derivatives,
  identify modified output as modified, and do not present Splashery's generated geometry as an
  official NASA product. Do not extend these findings to every NASA-hosted third-party file.

## What was verified

- The current repository instructions, owner's relevant notes, and shelf names were read.
- All 45 listed entries have a linked live license basis; NC and SA are marked separately.
- Direct public file links, source sizes, dimensions, and durations were recorded without
  credentials.
- GLB headers, mesh indices, textures, and extension lists were inspected for the four dedicated
  Studio files. Each is complete, under 10,000,000 bytes, textured, and free of Draco.
- Several other small GLBs and images were fetched and inspected; the notes distinguish those from
  metadata-only records and partial downloads. The two large castle/frieze files were only partially
  fetched. Wright Flyer CDN downloads timed out.
- A contact sheet was reviewed for the eight element photos, two depth stills, four model previews,
  and four landmark previews. Sampled Hardegg and El Kobba video frames were reviewed at 360p. El
  Kobba's cards and people are a confirmed cleanup issue; full-length playback of all video
  candidates was not performed.
- Some Wikimedia media requests returned 429 or a robot-policy response. Those transfers were not
  forced or treated as successful. Live license/file metadata remains the evidence for those rows.
- No splat training, finished conversion, scientific/mechanical validation, browser import QA, or
  release-ready asset clearance is claimed. This is a source report with explicit handoff checks.
