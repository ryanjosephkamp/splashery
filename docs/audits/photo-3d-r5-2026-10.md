## Target selection and correction

The [118-input atlas](photo-3d-failures-2026-10.md) supplies the frozen source hashes, nine poses,
two profiles, probes, metric definitions, and review levels. A row is one input at one profile. For
each class, cost is the flagged row count times its mean severity, where severity is the row's worst
supported pose score divided by that class's existing review level. Equivalently, cost is the sum of
these ratios over flagged rows. This is a repair-priority heuristic, not a perceptually calibrated
comparison between unlike units. The initial ranking omitted the atlas's separate flat-depth warning
filter. Including that filter corrects the three highest costs as follows. This correction does not
move the original deadline; no production reconstruction change was retained under the initial
selection.

| ------------------------------------ | -----------------------: | ------------------------: | -------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| Directional ribbons in revealed fill |                211 / 223 | 40.3048 percentage points | 566.9536 | Continue eligible background texture into disocclusions while preserving gradients and believable content.                             |

The remaining costs are texture-energy loss 232.1019, fringe width 195.4848, lost thin-line contrast
