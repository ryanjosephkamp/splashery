# Video to 3D on real clips: task 88

This audit is in progress. The acceptance goal has not been established. Codex started the bounded
12-hour run on October 15, 2026, at 04:32:25 UTC; the limit is 16:32:25 UTC. The frozen baseline is

## Corpus and licenses

[The manifest](../../tools/v3d-corpus.json) freezes 20 distinct real source uploads, their accepted
5–12-second spans, authors, licenses, transformations, and SHA-256 checksums. The set was completed
before candidate quality measurements. The accepted clips total 26,807,550 bytes. No generated
footage, synthetic camera movement, exposure changes, stabilization, or sharpening was added.
Preparation trims, removes audio, scales to 480 pixels high, and encodes H.264 at 24 fps.

Coverage includes pets, plants, statues, a church interior, a lens chamber, an indoor scene, a
residential street, genuine camera translation and orbits, a still camera, shaky movement, a fast
pan, and unaltered low light. No people are the subject. Eight frames per accepted span were
inspected for identifiable people and brands; source, license, and inspection receipts are retained
with the media. Each derivative retains its source's license and attribution.

This is an exploratory corpus, not a representative survey of phone recordings. Useful translation
comes largely from aerial footage. A ground-level phone orbit around a small tabletop object and a
busy pedestrian street are absent; source hardware is usually unknown. These limitations must stay
visible when interpreting the results.

## What changed

- The phone's 1,200-step run can now refine before its iteration budget ends; the old first
  refinement threshold was 1,500 steps. A run limited by elapsed time can still stop before the new
  iteration threshold.
- Conservative surface initialization replaces some jittered sparse clones with oriented points
  inside local sparse triangles. A second recorded camera must support their color. It uses the
  existing seed ceiling; it is an initialization prior, not a dense depth reconstruction.
- New video training uses coordinate-median seed bounds while retaining every sparse point. On the
  cached plant solve, one point about 1,447 units away inflated the mean-centered P90 radius from
  about 0.454 to 2.482. The new opt-in stabilizes scale bounds and position learning rates; legacy
  initialization remains byte-identical when omitted. Seven CPU tests passed, including captured
  original parameter hashes. This is not evidence of a native quality gain.
- New custom bakes retain fitted surfaces through the viewer's photometric cleanup path. Existing
  baked samples keep their own metadata and tiers.
- New bakes carry their black training background into the viewer. The fitted view restores the
  prior camera clear color when disposed, while subsequent host color choices remain authoritative.
  Existing samples without the new metadata retain their existing background behavior.
- Preview buffers fit the actual training and preview sizes. The phone's GPU fence-ring depth is
  reduced from four to two, and a final batch cannot exceed the requested iteration count. Revision
  7 also bounds software-adapter submissions to two steps and one outstanding fence; physical phones
  retain two fences. Camera refinement yields to the UI. Native training sizes, iteration budgets,
  and optimizer settings are unchanged. Existing session wall-time counters are summarized in new
  bake metadata to diagnose encoding, fencing, and metric readbacks.
- Training photographs wait for the seek's presented frame and retain its actual media timestamp. A
  paused same-frame seek can briefly advance the muted local video to recover an actual
  presentation, then pauses again; it never invents a timestamp. The fast suitability survey retains
  its calibrated seeked-frame sampling and its existing 4.5-second deadline.
- An exact serial tile sort is selected on software adapters. The original parallel shader and
  hardware default are unchanged. Browser checks cover unsigned depth/id order, ties, empty and
  single-entry tiles, the 2,048-entry boundary, large padding, and canaries. Complete entry,
  gradient, output, and loss bytes match; three real optimizer steps export identical finite PLYs. A
  small fixed-input timing probe supports this experiment, without establishing end-to-end gains.
  Software adapters also select the engine's existing direct gradient path. Two reversed-order
  probes on the same real 15,000-point, 320×180 input preserved complete parameter, image, loss, and
  tile-list bytes after six steps. Direct-path warm steps took 2.43 versus 3.61 seconds and 3.22
  versus 4.76 seconds under overlapping load. The probes used a 45,000-splat allocation ceiling,
  rather than the native phone's 30,000; they establish neither native bake timing nor
  physical-device gains. The browser regression additionally checks exact raster, gradient, PLY, and
  three-step parameter parity across both sorts and both gradient paths.
- Dark input receives specific lighting advice. Camera solving, decoding, GPU setup, finishing, and
  export have finite deadlines and recovery advice. Training requests Finish after eight minutes on
  the phone setting or 15 minutes on other settings; in-flight work, finishing, export, and other
  stages add time before a bake completes. Absolute elapsed-time checks also reject work that
  returns after its deadline while blocking JavaScript's timer queue. They cannot make a blocked
  thread display its explanation punctually. The progress estimate respects the remaining training
  limit and explains when finishing begins.
- Sessions terminate worker pools and discard GPU initialization resources that arrive after
  disposal. Phone pause and resume share one asynchronous frame loop, and Finish joins it before the
  final readback. The separate `Engine:` commits preserve existing initialization when the new seed
  ceiling is omitted. Late continuations after recovery or disposal now check the originating
  trainer before readback, view updates, metrics, and GPU fences.

The cleanup-only diagnostic was insufficient: on the original cat probe it increased SSIM from
0.31271 to 0.32528 but reduced Laplacian variance from 0.00007991 to 0.00004755. It cannot establish
the clarity goal. The surface initializer produced 2,636 supported points from 614 sparse points in
about 467 ms in a separate real-clip diagnostic; that is an initialization measurement, not a

## Measurement protocol

`tools/v3d-bench.mjs` uses the actual toy file input and native Auto settings: phone at 390×844 with
a mobile user agent, and desktop at 1440×900. It does not substitute a cached bake, reduce the
training settings, or press Finish now for acceptance. The smallest native span setting that covers
the whole clip is selected. The phone uses the real `phone` tier; desktop uses `high`.

The first-view timer ends after the progressive 3D preview's submitted GPU work completes. Bake time
includes input assessment and the normal toy completion. Memory is sampled aggregate RSS of the
complete browser process tree, with shared pages potentially counted more than once; it is not GPU
RAM. A host watchdog bounds setup, baking, evaluation, and browser teardown. A watchdog expiration
is an incomplete measurement, never a clear product rejection.

Every fourth baseline camera interval supplies a held-out source frame near its midpoint. All
planned training seek attempts are excluded. An independent decoder presents exact source frames and
reproduces the training extraction canvas and JPEG encoder. Every selected training JPEG must match
that decoder; each held-out JPEG must be distinct from every training JPEG. Repeated encoding must
be deterministic. FFmpeg selects the exact verified source index for the scoring reference. The
production viewer interpolates the recorded camera path; no image fitting, mask, crop to a favorable
region, post-sharpening, or camera alignment is applied. The sole crop is the existing panorama
guard, frozen from baseline for both revisions.

PSNR, Gaussian-window luminance SSIM, and Laplacian variance reuse task 55's arithmetic. Both
profiles require the ratio of corpus median rendered Laplacian variances to reach 1.5 and the
difference in corpus median SSIM to reach 0.03. Only paired successful baseline clips receive image
scores. Explained rejections have no image score; a candidate cannot earn a gain by dropping a
baseline success. Raw partial scores remain visible, but missing outcomes, mismatched references, or
incomplete evidence block an acceptance claim.

The runner fingerprints actual served source bytes, browser binary and arguments, tools, settings,
manifest, input bytes, and reference evidence. Resume rejects changes. Incremental baseline appends
are allowed; each candidate row binds its exact immutable baseline row, model, evaluation, and PNG
receipts. Retries archive previous evidence. The six-sample check freezes every held-out view at
both tiers and requires exact rendered-pixel preservation; it verifies existing bakes and viewer
behavior, rather than retraining their original captures.

Split profile collections can be joined without launching a browser:

```sh
node tools/v3d-bench-join.mjs tools/v3d-corpus.json \
  /workspace/v3d-r4-media/main-joined-snapshot-01 \
  /workspace/v3d-r4-media/main-r1 \
  /workspace/v3d-r4-media/main-desktop-r1
```

The destination must be fresh, outside product checkouts, and separate from every source. The tool
requires matching manifest, full cohort, and exact provenance; it preserves each captured row, its
complete case directory, and the original captured `run.json` bytes. Source runners may append rows
while the tool copies, but every captured row and case must remain unchanged. Unlisted or
in-progress cases and unrelated archived attempts are excluded. `join.json` records the byte
inventories and coverage; missing outcomes and failures stay visible. A join does not establish
quality, acceptance, or isolated timing gains. Existing per-row candidate reference bindings remain
valid because their row and artifact fingerprints are preserved.

## Environment and limits

The workspace has no physical GPU. It provides a four-core CPU-time quota and software WebGPU
through Chromium 151.0.7922.173 and SwiftShader. These are emulated profile measurements, not
physical-phone measurements. The runner also supports native GPU launches for reproduction on
hardware. Start-time system load averages are recorded for each attempt; they do not measure ongoing
concurrency or CPU-quota throttling. Main, candidate, and regression work overlapped, so these
timings cannot establish an isolated no-slower result. The regression suite runs with Node.js
22.15.0, Chrome for Testing 141.0.7390.37, Inter 4.1, Xvfb, and Mesa llvmpipe, following the
existing tests' runtime requirements. Tool notices are in [LICENSES.md](../../LICENSES.md).

The initial six-clip diagnostic predates the strict protocol. Its memory sampler counted only the
browser parent, so its RSS numbers are invalid. Those artifacts are retained as diagnostics and
excluded from acceptance. The fresh run is `main-r1`; candidate iterations retain their own output
directories and source fingerprints.

The `candidate-r2` cat-plays phone attempt produced its first preview at 109.5 seconds but failed
while finishing at 617.2 seconds, without a model or held-out image scores. Main produced its first
preview at 69.6 seconds and completed at 875.8 seconds. The failed candidate cannot count as a speed
improvement, and the overlapping software-GPU runs do not establish isolated timing gains. A CPU
reproduction confirmed that pause and resume could overlap asynchronous training frames and that
Finish could read metrics and emit completion before those frames settled. This scheduler defect was
corrected before `candidate-r3`; its contribution to the particular r2 timeout was not isolated. The
earlier candidate artifacts remain separate from the current run.

The `candidate-r3` cat-plays result finished in 614.7 seconds, compared with main's 875.8, but its
SSIM gain of 0.01134 and sharpness ratio of 0.432 both missed the goal. Narrower, denser surface
seeds in `candidate-r4` raised sharpness to 3.346 times main's and SSIM by 0.00442 on this one clip.
Its first preview was slower (162.3 versus 69.6 seconds), and bake time was 645.7 seconds. These are
partial, overlapping software-GPU measurements, not a corpus acceptance result. Earlier candidates
remain preserved as negative evidence.

The public product branch also includes subsequent `main` through `d33f9421`. The benchmark baseline
remains pinned to the task-start revision, and candidate source fingerprints identify precisely
which runtime each result measured.

The integrated-main `candidate-r5` cat run finished in 707.5 seconds with SSIM +0.00195 and
sharpness 3.242 times main's; its first preview took 236.4 seconds. The separate `candidate-r6`
experiment used a plane-intersection Jacobian to correct foreshortened surface footprints. Its cat
result finished in 690.9 seconds, but sharpness fell to 0.741 times main's and SSIM rose only
0.00251. That experiment has not established a quality gain and is not the draft's default runtime.
The apparent sharpness improvement in r4/r5 includes visible thin streaks, rather than only
recovered texture. The CPU diagnostic validates covariance arithmetic, not a reconstruction-quality
result.

The fitted training background now survives routine exposure or accent refreshes while a genuine

## Supplemental evaluation of stored timeout models

A bake can save its PLY, camera metadata, and hash-bound independent source-frame receipts before
its host deadline interrupts scoring. `tools/v3d-bench-supplement.mjs` snapshots that complete raw
case and captured run before any replay. It accepts only evaluation-stage host-deadline errors,
checks every available original binding, and leaves the strict row and censored timing unchanged. An
optional independently saved snapshot also checks the currently stored metadata bytes.

`model.json` was not hashed in the original benchmark row. The supplementary snapshot newly binds
its exact bytes and uses its stored poses unchanged; this is not historical metadata-byte
verification. Every training JPEG and held-out JPEG must again match the original independent pixel
receipts, and source references use the frozen exact FFmpeg frame indices. Any original saved render
PNG must reproduce exactly. A case without original render PNGs is explicitly a new read-only
evaluation, not verification of an original capture.

Prepare the CPU-only immutable snapshot separately from a replay:

```sh
node tools/v3d-bench-supplement.mjs prepare tools/v3d-corpus.json \
  /workspace/v3d-r4-media/corpus /workspace/v3d-r4-media/main-r1 \
  /workspace/v3d-r4-media/supplement-main-r1-pisac-phone-r1 \
  --id=pisac --profile=phone --cleanup=main

# Run separately from timed bakes, with the same frozen served source.
node tools/v3d-bench-supplement.mjs render \
  /workspace/v3d-r4-media/supplement-main-r1-pisac-phone-r1 \
  --url=http://127.0.0.1:4174/ --timeout=600000
```

The derived report is a supplemental quality diagnostic. It cannot recover censored timing, turn an
original harness error into a pass, fill missing strict cohort outcomes, or bypass the existing
comparison and review gates. A supplementary movie would require its own explicit diagnostic label
and the same source/provenance and pixel checks. Preparing or unit-testing this tool is not evidence
that a real browser replay succeeded.

The Pisac phone replay completed with SSIM 0.55978164, Laplacian variance 0.00012631448, and PSNR
15.2923 dB. All 16 training JPEG receipts and four held-out source receipts were independently
reproduced. The original row remains an evaluation-stage harness error with censored timing; there
were no original render PNGs to verify. These are supplemental scores, excluded from strict
acceptance. The images still show substantial blur and color artifacts.

## Rejected geometry and depth experiments

Revision 6 replaced the surface initializer's approximate world-space footprint with the exact local
perspective Jacobian and rejected highly grazing planes. An independent projected-covariance check
passed, but actual held-out results regressed: cat sharpness was 0.7412 times main's, with SSIM up
only 0.00251; plant sharpness was about 0.659 times main's, with SSIM down 0.05380. This footprint
experiment is excluded from the product default. Correct covariance alone did not establish better
reconstruction.

A separate four-frame probe ran the already-vendored depth model through its dedicated worker. Every
anchor failed the fixed minimum sparse-depth-spread gate before robust affine calibration or
third-camera RGB validation. It proposed zero points and measured no quality gain. This does not
prove that calibration is mathematically impossible; it provides no evidence to integrate this model
into Video to 3D. The probe's raw inference times, model and WASM byte receipts, fixed
configuration, output maps, and failures are retained separately from timed corpus bakes.

A deterministic plane-fit probe accepted no plane: its largest refined support was 43 of 614 points,
below the fixed 20% gate. It proposed no dense points. The same investigation identified the distant
point's disproportionate effect on the seed radius; changing the bounds does not turn the rejected
plane into accepted geometry.

The first structural-loss experiment was invalid: its backward shader required nine storage bindings
on the default eight-binding device. The failed run and all errors are retained; its structural-loss
scores cannot be used. An `Engine:` fix removes the unused camera-gradient binding for this path. A
real browser regression now trains and exports at the default limit, with improved fit and exact
shared/direct parameters after 20 steps. The real-input quality probe is rerunning; structural loss
is not enabled in the product without supporting results.
