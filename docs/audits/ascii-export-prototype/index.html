What this demonstrates

The converter averages each cell’s pixels, accounts for transparent pixels, and maps brightness to a character. Repeating that step over a captured sequence produces the animation. Color is optional; the underlying text stays the same.

Grapes preserve the bunch and falling fruit clearly. The orange keeps its moving wedges. The strawberry is a useful harder case: its outline survives, but fine seeds and subtle texture lose detail. Color can help distinguish those regions.

These are recorded examples from earlier source revisions, not live toys from current main. This prototype does not add an export-menu option. A later integration needs a capture adapter and encoding step; PDF and text QR work remain separate.
