# Open science data for Splashery

Checked October 5, 2026 (UTC). Research only. Prepared by dotio directly; no Codex or Work
delegation.

## Summary

1. This report adds 28 catalog sets and five feed entries to the earlier source audit.
2. Twenty-four catalog sets have an allowed license basis; four stay in the Unclear table.
3. The feed section adds two feed-only products, for 30 distinct products overall.
4. Start with nonhuman PDB files, CIE tables, and the small houseplant splat.
5. HYG and OpenNGC make useful sky labs, but they do not supply galaxy geometry.
6. The Kepler sample contains candidates and false positives, not just confirmed planets.
7. Berkeley Earth is NC; the CIE tables and star catalogs carry SA obligations.
8. Keep the early Scripps segment out of the NOAA-only CO2 sample until separately cleared.
9. Atomic emission lines, orbital elements, and the SPZ example still need license decisions.
10. Large archives need offline cropping; this report does not claim they run in a browser
    unchanged.

## Scope and evidence

I read [the earlier audit](new-sources-2026-10.md), the main-branch Ground rules in
[CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md), and the push notes. None of that audit's 37 dataset rows is relisted
here. New datasets from the same publisher are identified separately. In particular, GeoNet replaces
another listing of the already-covered USGS earthquake feed; the NOAA choices are not the earlier
CO-OPS, NEXRAD, ERSST, or ECMWF products.

All license links below were opened live on October 5, 2026. Quotes reproduce the relevant license
text or exact structured license field. A short identifier such as `cc-by-4.0` is quoted as an
identifier, not presented as a sentence from a human-facing page. **D / C** means whether adaptation
and commercial use are allowed under the stated license. BY requires credit and a license link;
identify changes. **SA** means keep the adapted asset under the same license. **NC** means no
commercial use and requires `nc: true` in the asset registry. Never combine an NC asset and a BY-SA
asset into a single asset. These are copyright findings, not a guarantee about every other right or
the scientific accuracy of the source.

Sizes are exact source bytes, not browser memory requirements. “Fetched” means the small file was
retrieved, not that its visualization was tested. Large-file sizes come from live publisher
metadata; the multi-gigabyte archives were not downloaded. Dynamic endpoints will change size.
Unless a row says otherwise, its published HTTPS download needs no account or API key; metadata
verification alone does not prove that every large transfer will finish. Direct-download links use
stable source routes, not temporary signed redirects. No personal or patient dataset is selected.
Repository contacts and scientific author credits are provenance, not toy data.

## Five live feeds

These are five on-demand options, not a background polling plan. All five returned HTTP 200 and
`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` during this check. That header is evidence for a keyless browser
GET, not a browser integration test or a permanent service guarantee. The NWS identification
requirement needs special care below. Keep a dated local snapshot, show source and data time, and
offer refresh only on request. Do not send a person's location or other personal data. The two
feed-only products below bring the report to 30 distinct products, still within 25–35.

## Handoff notes for the lanes

- **Small first:** ship the houseplant capture, the three PDB files, CIE CSVs, and a small selected
  star sample before any multi-gigabyte archive. The PDB live fetch is expressly approved in the
  current Ground rules; select nonhuman examples by default.
- **Honest splats:** a point cloud can be shown with small Gaussian kernels without becoming a
  trained photoreal Gaussian scene. Atoms, stars, spectra, and simulated fluid samples are
  scientific marks. Keep that distinction visible in names and help text.
- **Formats still missing:** the cleared examples cover ordinary PLY/LAS and real `.splat`. I did
  not verify an allowed, ready-made `.spz` or `.sog` asset. Convert the cleared houseplant offline
  into those formats, preserve its BY credit, and test round trips. Do not relabel a file extension
  or treat an MIT software license as an asset license. No conversion was done for this research
  report.
- **Translation lab:** the Dryad CDS download was blocked in this environment. Resolve access, then
  choose short complete plant CDS records and compare their translated products against the matching
  amino-acid file. Show strand, reading frame, introns versus CDS, ambiguous bases, and stop codons.
  Do not imply that protein sequence uniquely determines DNA or final 3D shape.
- **Spectrum gap:** CIE and QM9S are useful, cleared alternatives for color and molecular-spectrum
  labs. They do not fill the atomic-emission-line gap. NIST ASD remains withheld until its rights
  fit the allowlist or the owner changes the policy.
- **Source hygiene:** keep original ID/version, direct URL, checked date, attribution, license,
  source hash, and every crop/downsample/conversion in the eventual asset record. Do not add logos,
  brand names, copyrighted illustrations, or personal fields from surrounding pages.
- **Scientific checks:** retain coordinate systems, units, error bars, missing flags, and time
  bases. Choose float origins before loading large geospatial coordinates. Label simulations, model
  forecasts, photogrammetry, and observations separately.
- **Access is not licensing:** a public download can have unusable terms; a properly licensed file
  can still have a blocked download. The Unclear table and the Dryad access warning keep those cases
  separate. I excluded the York Minster response set because its metadata and description disagree
  about BY versus BY-NC-SA; I did not resolve that conflict by picking the more permissive notice.

## Verification and limits

Read-only source research used the GitHub connection, public web pages, publisher APIs, and small
public file requests. No paid source, account creation, token request, personal dataset, patient
dataset, training run, or third-party upload was needed. The actual license text was checked live;
no generic “government data is free” assumption cleared NIST or Horizons. Large archives were
assessed from metadata, not decoded or visually inspected. This report does not claim successful
end-to-end browser loading, feed integration, model training, or scientific validation. The proposed
repository change is this Markdown file only.
