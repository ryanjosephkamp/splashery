# Bases audit, October 2026

Lane Sharpness B (Opus 5.5), October 2, 2026. Every toy on every shelf (labs included) was turned
over and rendered from below at 390×844 (a 3x phone, the mid tier), and each underside was checked:
is it closed and solid the way the real object would be, or hollow or see-through? The owner's list
from his review of October 2 came first. A scan's underside is closed with hard-edged, kit-built
pieces (rig add-ons in `src/rigs.js`), never by smearing the capture.

"Fine" means solid from below (or open the way the real thing is). Toys marked FIXED have a
before-and-after card from below on Effect review page 2 (lane SharpB, "Closed bases").
