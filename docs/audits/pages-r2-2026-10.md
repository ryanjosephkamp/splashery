rendered Laplacian variance divided by the source's variance at the displayed size. Raw variance is
retained too, and the phone text check also requires its raw variance to improve by at least 30%.

more.

software GPU, without counting shared pages repeatedly.

## Implementation

- An opt-in continuous picture face sits over the existing Gaussian paper and binding. It uses the
  same circular-arc leaf equation and rigid part transforms in both WebGL2 and WebGPU. Shared
  vertices and opaque depth writes keep each face connected throughout a turn.
- PDF paper retains inset coarse splats, without allocating a full hidden raster of ink splats. The
  continuous face supplies its exact perimeter; coarse Gaussian edge strips protruded at the corners
  in phone review, so connected PDF pages omit those redundant strips. The phone Picture lab uses
  fixed coarse paper beneath its sharp image face, as the album does. Larger tiers retain
  alternating Gaussian color samples within half the original pixel allowance; the face retains its
  full resolution. Native images keep their original colored-plane placement. Mounted album photos
  use fixed coarse paper backing, saving the duplicate detail work beneath their opaque faces. The
  Gaussian backing remains within its original pixel budget, and the face's texture has a separate
  bound.
- Documents first render at their projected width, then upgrade to 1.5 times that width within the
  existing width cap, over fixed 128-pixel-wide Gaussian paper. This keeps the backing count and
  container stable while source detail upgrades. A one-level texture bias avoids filtering already
  antialiased letters twice, while mipmaps and anisotropic sampling remain available as a page
  turns.
- Strictly opaque grayscale pages store each original 8-bit value in one texture channel. The
  fragment shader repeats that value into RGB without quantization. A full-raster check preserves
  even tiny colored marks and transparent pixels through the full-color path. Two-color ink uses a
  coverage channel only when every source channel can be reconstructed within one 8-bit step;
  unrelated colors and transparency retain RGBA.
- The first face installs before a moving opening camera requests its next resolution; normal
  settled-view upgrades then provide full detail. This avoids discarding the first readable image.
- Source color compaction overlaps the paper worker, using independent pixel buffers instead of
  serializing both jobs before installation. The shared worker starts while media decodes. Backing
  resamples request a CPU-readable canvas at creation, avoiding a GPU upload/readback round trip
  before the paper job can start.
- Decoded single images supply their raster synchronously to the continuous-face builder, so the
  paper worker starts before that frame submits GPU work. Existing consumers retain the original
  promise-returning drawing method. The new check compares both crop paths byte for byte.
- Native images first install within the original pixel allowance, then upgrade to 1.5 times their
  displayed reading width within the source and device caps. Native images and colored PDF faces
  upload exact RGBA bytes and release their staging canvas. The cache keeps only the latest
  resolution of each native image, instead of duplicate Gaussian buffers for the intermediate sizes
  crossed during an opening glide.
- Flat, rigid faces use one quad and a shorter vertex shader. Curved leaves keep their connected
  grid, and raised relief retains its denser surface.
- Connected faces render their exact printed front and white reverse. Kit covers render afterward
  with depth testing, retaining cover occlusion. Hidden Gaussian sheet data no longer creates a
  second splat work buffer or render pass. This addresses the loading and memory cost exposed by the
  three-pass trial, while keeping the connected sheet and its original binding. Both shader backends
  use their real front-facing built-in, including a pickable reverse.
- Layer masks use exact binary decisions and nearest sampling. Masked cutouts have no soft Gaussian
  backing that could protrude past their silhouettes.
- Image-file charts and text use graphic classification instead of unconditionally running photo
  inference. Graphic masks use the raised picture's detail, and image crops are reused.
- Cache accounting includes actual buffer allocations, source canvases, and retained relief maps. A
  discarded prefetch is attempted once per request, rather than rebuilt repeatedly when installed
  pages fill the cache. The Picture lab prepares its next PDF page on demand; the book retains
  lookahead.
- Your book, Picture lab, and Photo album opt in. The Storybook and Picture frame retain their
  existing rendering; GIF and video sheets retain their live screen path. The three changed toys
  identify their pages as hybrid in their input notes, and show this MIT contribution in their
  existing in-app credit drawer.
- The final resolution cap remains reachable when zooming a supersampled page, even if its last
  detail step is smaller than the normal rebuild threshold. The unchanged phone zoom spec caught and
  verified this behavior.
- Shader preparation executes monochrome and RGBA mipmapped materials in a private 16×16 render
  target during media loading, then releases the camera, layer, target, and textures after the
  submitted frame. Clipped triangles left fragment preparation until the first visible page.
  Programs are marked warm only after submission and remain in the graphics device's cache. Both
  render backends also write the renderer's normal face ID and depth in picking passes.
- Replaced faces and their GPU resources are destroyed with their sheets. Switching toys hides their
  faces immediately while the existing retirement queue releases the old resources.

The album carries over sharp continuous photo faces, bounded Gaussian backing, connected page
motion, exact cutout silhouettes, and cleanup. PDF text extraction and ink-paper processing do not
apply to its image set. Its order, aspect grouping, mounts, corners, captions, cover materials,
navigation, and heavier pull behavior are retained.
