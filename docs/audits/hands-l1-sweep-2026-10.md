## Measurement rules and limits

The driver uses the existing switch and Reset handlers, the actual center GPU pick,
`HandsOn.pressAt`, `moveTo` and `release`. It snaps to each toy's default camera and pauses the
render-driven player clock before stepping `HandsOn.step(1/60)`. No gravity, mass, damping,
collision or sleep settings are changed. A pick miss stays a failed `center-miss` check; a synthetic
center press only supplies downstream diagnostic numbers. Toys whose switch cannot turn on or whose
existing controls reject whole-toy grabs are not forced into a physics world.

The drag lifts 126 CSS pixels over 66 simulation frames, then moves right 72 pixels over six frames.
Lift must exceed 0.15 R and release must retain at least 0.4 R/s sideways velocity. Every completed
XPBD substep during the hold and eight-second flight/rest window checks finite state and the full
collision shape against the floor, four walls and ceiling. Signed gaps below −0.01 R fail, with a
0.000001 R floating-point margin. This numerical tolerance allows small constraint residuals; it is
not a claim of mathematically zero penetration. Bounds during the Reset interpolation are not
audited.

Landing is the first post-release collider clearance ≤0.01 R. Settling requires engine sleep by
eight seconds and, over the last 0.5 seconds, ≤0.01 R displacement and ≤0.02 radians rotation. Rest
collider clearance must lie between −0.01 R and 0.03 R. Independently, up to 6,000 original splat
centers transformed with the final body pose supply a 0.5th-percentile exterior height, allowing
−0.05 R to 0.10 R. This catches support mismatch without reusing the collider as the only oracle; it
excludes Gaussian extent, shader deformation and visibility masks. Rest-height rows therefore
warrant a visible-geometry reproduction before changing toy art or physics. Control-path causes are
directly observed. Collider and sleep explanations are candidates from inspected code, not confirmed
per-toy root causes.

Reset is checked for the full two seconds, including position error <0.001 R, quaternion angular
error <0.001 radians, cleared moved state and restored entity transform. Soft toys must produce a
nonzero squish uniform peak ≥0.005 after release. Upward velocity after contact is recorded as
`bounce`, without imposing one bounce height on every material. Nominally heavy and soft samples
cover intended toy families; this audit does not certify material realism. All measured bodies in
this baseline use mass 1 ([`hands-on.js:182`](../../src/physics/hands-on.js#L182)).

Browser `pageerror` events and error-level console messages are collected from startup, loading,
picking and simulation. N/A means the control path was blocked or the measurement was unavailable;
it never means a passing zero.
