Tools

Every toy answers to the same tools. Pick one in the gallery's toolbar, then drag on the toy.

Play

Orbit 1
Drag to turn the toy; pinch or scroll to zoom. Double-tap to reset the view.
Poke 2
Tap or drag to push splats in; they spring back.
Paint 3
Brush color onto the toy, splat by splat.
Magnet 4
Pull splats toward your finger and let them fly back.
Clay 5
Push and pull the surface like soft clay.
Tap
Each toy has its own tap: a peel, a hop, a song, a shatter.

Make and share

Make
Build your own toy from a shape, a palette and a seed.
Link
A link carries the toy and every setting, so others see what you see.
Embed
Put a live toy on your own page with an iframe or one script tag.
GIF and video
Save a turntable or a short effect loop as a file.
