Tools

Splashery is also a workshop. Each of these opens in the gallery and works on your own things, on your device; nothing is uploaded.

  • Make and convert
  • Open your own files
  • Scan and measure
  • QR codes

  • In every toy

Make and convert

Turn a photo, a video, a model or a song into splats you can play with.

  • Photo to 3D labsA photo becomes a relief with real depth; tap to lift it out of the picture.Open it
  • Moving photo to 3D labsA GIF or a short video, with depth, still moving and still making its sound.Open it
  • Video to 3D labsFilm a scene and fly through it as splats, solved and trained on your device.Open it
  • Model to splats labsA 3D model (glTF, OBJ or STL) becomes splats that lift off and settle back.Open it
  • Song landscape labsA song becomes a landscape you fly over: a real spectrogram, in 3D.Open it

Open your own files

Pages, pictures, molecules and crystals, opened right in your browser.

  • Splat filesDrop a .ply, .sog, .splat or .spz file in the gallery's Toy tab to look at it, play with it and share it.Open the gallery
  • Picture lab labsA PDF, a picture, a GIF or a video, drawn in splats with the figures lifted off the page.Open it
  • Your book labsAny PDF as a book with pages that turn, curl and pop their pictures out.Open it
  • Photo album labsA set of photos as an album: tap to turn a page, pull one over, pop a photo out.Open it
  • Picture frame labsPut your own photo, GIF or video in a frame that swings on its nail.Open it
  • MoleculeType a name, a formula or a SMILES string, or open a MOL, SDF, XYZ or PDB file.Open it
  • Thermal ellipsoids labsOpen a crystal file (CIF) and see each atom's jiggle as a soft ellipsoid.Open it

Scan and measure

See inside things, and look closer than an eye can: X-ray, CT, MRI and microscopes.

  • Airport X-ray scanner labsSend bags through the scanner and watch their X-ray pictures build up.Open it
  • How CT works labsA CT scan, step by step: scan a shell, then cut into it.Open it
  • Walnut CT scan labsA real CT scan of a walnut: drag to cut into it, slice by slice.Open it
  • MRI of a fruit labsScroll through an MRI of a fruit, or play through every slice.Open it
  • Electron microscope labsZoom in on a sample, step by step, the way an electron microscope does.Open it
  • Super-resolution microscope labsReal super-resolution microscopy data, down to single molecules.Open it
  • Thermal camera labsA thermal camera that watches a cup of tea cool.Open it
  • Room echo meter labsClap once, and the microphone measures how long your room echoes.Open it

QR codes

Make one, see how it works, break it on purpose, and hide three in one.

  • QR code labsType a link, pick a style, and check that the code still scans.Open it
  • How a QR code works labsLight up each part of a code, or encode your own text step by step.Open it
  • QR damage lab labsScratch, smudge and cover a code, and watch how much it can lose.Open it
  • Three QR codes in one labsThree codes in one picture: pull them apart and read each.Open it

Sound and light

Hear a toy, see a sound, and see yourself in splats. These ask for the microphone or the camera only when you tap.

  • Chladni plate labsBow a plate, or sing to it, and sand finds the still lines of the sound.Open it
  • Song landscape labsPlay a song and fly over its sound; or use the microphone to see it live.Open it
  • Splat mirror labsTap Start camera and see yourself made of splats; turn it to see the depth.Open it
  • Room echo meter labsMeasure your room's echo with one clap.Open it
  • Screen labsA screen that plays your own picture or video, with a real off switch.Open it

In every toy

Orbit 1
Drag to turn the toy; pinch or scroll to zoom. Double-tap to reset the view.
Poke 2
Tap or drag to push splats in; they spring back.
Paint 3
Brush color onto the toy, splat by splat.
Magnet 4
Pull splats toward your finger and let them fly back.
Clay 5
Push and pull the surface like soft clay.
Tap
Each toy has its own tap: a peel, a hop, a song, a shatter.
