About this toy

A medicine ball is a heavy ball for exercise, used for throwing, catching and lifting to build strength. Most weigh from about 1 to 10 kilograms (2 to 20 pounds), and they are made of leather, rubber or tough cloth, often with a grippy surface.

Tap it and it is heaved up just a little, slowly, because it is heavy. It drops with a heavy thud and a big, slow squash, does not bounce at all, and a puff of dust spreads out across the floor.
