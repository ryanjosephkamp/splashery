About this toy

The Space Shuttle at launch, as splats from NASA's 3D model: the winged orbiter, the big orange external tank that fed its three main engines with liquid hydrogen and oxygen, and the two white solid rocket boosters. The Shuttles flew 135 missions from 1981 to 2011, carrying astronauts, satellites and the pieces of the International Space Station.

Tap to stage it in a flight's order, much faster than real: the boosters fall away about two minutes after launch, to be fished from the sea and used again; the main engines stop at about eight and a half minutes and the empty tank drops away, the only part not reused; the orbiter flies on with its two small maneuvering engines. The model's black and white tiles are drawn simply.

Is it right?

NASA's 3D model of the Space Shuttle at launch, cut into the orbiter, the external tank and the two boosters, colored, staging in a flight's order, much faster than real. Checked October 6, 2026.

  1. Correct The two boosters go first, about two minutes after launch; the external tank goes after the main engines stop, about eight and a half minutes in; the orbiter flies on with its two maneuvering engines.

    The drive moves each piece at its time on a 12-second clock; the test checks the order and that the boosters fall away to both sides.

    • Space Shuttle, Wikipedia: After expending their fuel, the SRBs were jettisoned approximately two minutes after launch... main engine cutoff (MECO), which occurred at T+8 minutes 30 seconds... the ET was jettisoned after main engine cutoff and just before orbit insertion, which used the orbiter's two Orbital Maneuvering System (OMS) engines.
    • Space Shuttle external tank, Wikipedia: The ET was jettisoned just over 10 seconds after main engine cut-off (MECO).

  2. Correct The Shuttles flew 135 missions from 1981 to 2011, and the external tank was the only major part not reused.

    About text.

    • Space Shuttle, Wikipedia: Five complete Space Shuttle orbiter vehicles were built and flown on a total of 135 missions from 1981 to 2011... The ET was the only major component of the Space Shuttle system that was not reused.

  3. Correct The model is NASA's (NASA/Michael D. Carbajal), used under NASA's media guidelines, and carries no logo.

    The model has no texture; the test checks the GLB has no image or texture. Its cockpit windows' frames (132 tiny meshes) are left out.

    The owner's call of October 6, 2026.

    • Images and media usage guidelines, NASA: NASA content (images, audio, video, and computer files used in the rendition of 3-dimensional models...) generally are not subject to copyright in the United States.

Where it simplifies

  • Staging that took minutes is shown in 12 seconds.
  • The flames are glowing cones.
  • The orbiter's black and white tiles are drawn simply: black below the wings, white above.
  • The boosters are not shown parachuting into the sea.
