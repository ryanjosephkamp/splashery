About this toy

The head and shoulders of a lioness of the extinct cave lion kind, captured from photos of a life-size model and drawn with splats. Cave lions lived in Europe and Asia in the Ice Age and were larger than lions today. Cave paintings show them without manes.

Tap it and it hops. Drag to circle it and look at the whiskers, the fur and the watching eyes.

Sources and credits

A real object, captured from photos and drawn with 3D Gaussian splats.

  • Scan: Lioness (Panthera Spelaea) by Spenser DIckerson, CC BY 4.0. Converted, decimated, recentred and scaled. One band of spherical harmonics kept on the full file.
