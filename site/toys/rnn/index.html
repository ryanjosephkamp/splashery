Recurrent network

Tap it to read a sentence one word at a time. Try the LSTM style in the Toy tab.

About this toy

A recurrent network, or RNN, reads a sequence, like the words of a sentence, one piece at a time. It keeps a memory called the hidden state: after each word, the state loops back in with the next word, so what it knows builds up as it reads. An LSTM (long short-term memory) adds gates that decide what to forget, what to take in and what to pass on.

Tap it: THE, CAT and SAT rise into the cell one at a time. The cell flashes, and the glowing orb, the hidden state, takes on the word's color mixed with what it carried and runs around the loop. In the Toy tab, pick the LSTM style to see its three gates open and shut like shutters, or a 3D model. This is a scripted sequence, not a trained sentence reader.
