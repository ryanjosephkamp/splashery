About this toy

The mandeltorus is a fractal: a shape whose small parts repeat the pattern of the whole, over and over, smaller and smaller. It is a piece of math art, made by a computer from a formula related to the Mandelbrot set and bent into a ring, or torus.

Tap it and its rings turn opposite ways in bands with a cyan glow, then lock back into place. The mathematician Benoit Mandelbrot made up the word "fractal" in 1975; fractal patterns turn up in nature too, in ferns, coastlines and snowflakes.
