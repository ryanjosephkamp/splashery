About this toy

This chest was made ahead of time and stored as ready-made Gaussian splats, the way a detailed model trained or baked in other software arrives. Each splat keeps its own place, three sizes, a turn, a color, an opacity, and the piece it belongs to, packed into 18 bytes. The page loads the size that fits your device: 30,000 splats or 100,001, or fewer still on a slow one.

Tap to open the lid. It is its own piece, so it swings on its hinge as one solid part and shows the gold inside.

Is it right?

The chest is a set of 3D Gaussian splats baked ahead of time and stored at 18 bytes each. The stored rotation, sizes, color, and opacity follow the standard 3D Gaussian splatting layout, and the round-trip error was measured on two trained splat files in the repository. The chest itself is a made-up object, not a measured one. Checked October 12, 2026.

  1. Correct Each splat keeps its own place, three sizes, a turn, a color, an opacity, and the piece it belongs to.

    The encoder writes the position, the three log sizes, the rotation quaternion, the degree-0 color, the opacity after the sigmoid, and a part id for every splat, in the fields a 3D Gaussian splatting file defines.

    Only the degree-0 color is kept; view-dependent color is dropped.

    • 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering, Kerbl, Kopanas, Leimkühler, and Drettakis, ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2023: Each Gaussian has a position, a covariance stored as a scale and a rotation quaternion, an opacity, and color as spherical harmonics.

  2. Correct A turn is packed into 32 bits by storing the three smallest parts of the quaternion.

    The largest component's index takes 2 bits and the other three take 10 bits each over plus or minus one over the square root of two; the largest is rebuilt from unit length. Random rotations come back within 0.25 degrees.

    • Snapshot Compression, Glenn Fiedler, Gaffer On Games, 2015: A unit quaternion can be sent as its three smallest components plus two bits naming the largest, which is recovered from the unit length.

  3. Not yet checked The page loads 30,000 splats or 100,001, or fewer still on a slow device.

    The splat budget picks the largest stored tier that fits; below the smallest tier, that tier's first splats are used, which the converter stores in an even random order.

    A behavior of this site, checked by its tests; there is no outside source to compare against.
