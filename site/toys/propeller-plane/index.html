About this toy

This propeller plane is a biplane: it has two sets of wings, one above the other, held together by struts and wires. Two wings give a lot of lift for a light, slow plane. The spinning propeller at the front pulls it through the air. The Wright brothers' Flyer made a sustained, controlled powered flight in 1903; it was a biplane too.

Tap it to loop the loop: it climbs, flies up and over on its back in a big circle, and comes out level again. Pick the colors of the body and the wings in the Toy tab.
