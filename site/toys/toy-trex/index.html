About this toy

A rubber toy Tyrannosaurus rex on a green base, captured from photos and drawn with splats. Real T. rex lived about 68 million years ago and had a bite strong enough to crush bone, but their arms were tiny. Scientists now think they were covered in scaly skin, perhaps with a few feathers.

Tap it and it hops. Drag to circle it and look at the painted stripes.

Sources and credits
