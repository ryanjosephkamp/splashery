About this toy

The oak is a big, broad tree with a thick trunk and wavy-edged leaves, and its seeds are acorns. Oaks grow slowly and can live for hundreds of years. A single old oak is home to hundreds of kinds of insects, birds and other animals.

Tap it and the crown rocks on its trunk, and leaves come loose one by one and flutter down to the grass; then fresh leaves open in their place. In the Toy tab, pick spring, summer, autumn or winter, or a Seed for a different tree. In winter some oaks keep a few dead brown leaves on their twigs, and those are the ones that fall.
