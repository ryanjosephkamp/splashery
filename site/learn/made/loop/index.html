The loop a lane follows

How a piece of work gets from an idea to the public site, drawn as a loop graph.

One lane, from the queue to the public site

Every lane follows the same loop. The Operator writes the brief and starts a worker. The worker builds, the full test run and the Operator's review of the clips send the work back until it is right, and then it waits at a gate: nothing the public already sees changes without the owner's "good". Going public is the owner's decision alone, and it is the last gate.
