New features

  • October 11, 2026

    Gallery is now Playground: opens the rubber duck in the Playground, in a new tab

    • The app's Gallery is now called the Playground: one button, every toy on one page. Old links and saved scenes keep working.
    • Every toy page now shows the day the toy arrived, and the toy lists have a Newest first view.

  • October 10, 2026

    Real rocks, fossils and the night sky (in the labs): opens the triceratops skull in the Playground, in a new tab

    • Photoreal captures of a Triceratops skull, a cone shell, meteorites, fossils and minerals, with parts that move as solid pieces.
    • A celestial globe and an armillary sphere.

  • October 9 to 10, 2026

    Hands-on toys: opens the enigma machine in the Playground, in a new tab

    • More toys you can pick up, drop and throw, and that land and settle like the real thing: food, nature, vehicles, landmarks, space, weather and the tiny world, plus music, medieval and holiday toys.
    • Clothing, an Enigma machine and sharper photo views.

  • October 6 to 8, 2026

    Real data: planets, maps, orbits and shapes in four dimensions (in the labs): opens the the real earth in the Playground, in a new tab

    • The real Moon, Mars, Earth, Mercury and Venus, real moons and stars, and the solar system on real orbits.
    • Real maps and measurements: the Grand Canyon, the sea floor, tides, hurricanes, earthquakes and stork migration.
    • Simulations and shapes: a ripple tank, a light bench, strange attractors, 4D shapes and a volume viewer.
    • More QR codes: a picture made of a QR code, QR codes from real things and other barcodes.

  • October 5, 2026

    A new site, a QR lab and ways to see inside things: opens the airport x-ray scanner in the Playground, in a new tab

    • A preview of a new Splashery site: a home page, one menu everywhere, search, and a copy that keeps working offline.
    • A QR code lab: see how a QR code works, damage one on purpose and watch how much it can lose.
    • New imaging toys: an airport X-ray scanner, how CT works, a real CT scan of a walnut, an MRI of a fruit, an electron microscope and a thermal camera.
    • The alarm clock now tells the real time.

  • October 4, 2026

    Toys you can pick up, and a gentler manual: opens the basketball in the Playground, in a new tab

    • Hands-on toys: pick up a basketball, a beach ball or a snow globe, drop it, and watch it land and settle.
    • The Fluid lab works on a phone.
    • The Tinkerer's Manual was rewritten for a newcomer.
    • Moving photo to 3D keeps the sound and the speed of your clip, and the splat mirror holds still again.

  • October 3, 2026

    A QR code made of splats: opens the qr code in the Playground, in a new tab

    • A QR code generator whose codes are made of splats, with a check that the code still scans.
    • Hands-on showcase toys and a small physics engine under them.
    • Pages in a book can carry links, and figures can pop out of the page.
    • Sharper trained splats on our own toys, and a piano bar with new rotation defaults.

  • October 2, 2026

    Real science data, a faucet and film-your-own 3D: opens the thermal ellipsoids in the Playground, in a new tab

    • Science toys: crystals with thermal ellipsoids that now work well on a phone, with pinch-and-scroll zoom.
    • The song landscape gained tilt, recording and playback controls; the Chladni plate's sand was fixed; Moving photo to 3D arrived.
    • A tappable faucet, water that reads as water and real lava in the Fluid lab.
    • Video to 3D now tells you what to film and why a result failed, and steers the flight.

  • October 1, 2026

    Use your microphone, camera or screen, and film a scene: opens the photo to 3d in the Playground, in a new tab

    • Toys can use the microphone, the camera or screen capture, but only when you tap to start.
    • Real recorded sounds for the first shelves of toys.
    • Focus mode, bigger panels and a drawing pad.
    • Photo to 3D opens your own photos, and an early version of Video to 3D arrived.

  • September 30, 2026

    Liquids, pianos and your photo in depth: opens the grand piano in the Playground, in a new tab

    • The Fluid lab: liquids, smoke, steam and flames made of splats.
    • Four keyboards with moving keys and mechanisms, songs and a song bar.
    • Photo to 3D turns a photo into a depth relief on your device.
    • Books with pages you tap and pull, and sharper screens with a real off switch.
    • Seven everyday objects with moving parts, and a sharper island in Worlds.

  • September 29, 2026

    The chemistry set, machines and a sharper picture: opens the turing machine in the Playground, in a new tab

    • Chemistry: a periodic table, all 118 elements in the atom toy and many more molecules, crystals and orbitals.
    • Machines that compute: a Turing machine, a difference engine, an Enigma machine and a Bombe.
    • Splats are drawn sharper by default, and a Detail setting picks how many a toy gets.
    • The Tinkerer's Manual and the lab notebook, the anatomy atlas with layers you peel, and the song landscape's Live view.
    • A short terms-of-use page in the About tab.

  • September 28, 2026

    Math, AI and your own pages: opens the graph plotter in the Playground, in a new tab

    • Math toys: a graph plotter, a surface plotter, circles and waves, Fourier circles and a Pythagoras proof.
    • A shelf of AI and computing toys where you watch the data move, including a network that reads a digit you draw.
    • Every toy got a how-to line and an About text.
    • PDFs, photos, GIFs and videos can be turned into splats, and a book shows any PDF with turning pages.

  • September 27, 2026

    Toys you can reach into: opens the puzzle cube in the Playground, in a new tab

    • The puzzle cube turns under your finger, the bricks build models, chess takes your moves and Newton's cradle balls can be dragged.
    • A real pencil and a real tin can, made from photos with an image-to-3D model.

  • September 23 to 26, 2026

    The first toy box: opens the chess set in the Playground, in a new tab

    • Sharper splats on phones and computers, and a phone shelf shown as a full grid.
    • A sound for every toy, and new tap effects for well over a hundred toys across the shelves: fruit, balls, space, atoms, gems, the tiny world, the body, nature and weather.
    • A chess set you can play and a laptop you can type on.
