Why splats

  • Real things, captured

    Hundreds of thousands of tiny, soft, colored splats make a photoreal scan of a real strawberry, a bee or a cactus.

  • Every splat can move

    So a toy can peel, ripple, shatter and come back together, not just spin.

  • A recipe for each toy

    A few lines say which parts move and how. The same recipes run the atoms, the planets and the games, and you can write your own.
