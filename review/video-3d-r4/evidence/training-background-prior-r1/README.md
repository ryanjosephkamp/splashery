Do not hardcode this color or relax thresholds for the source. A future generic implementation must estimate from that run's actual loss-eligible registered training RGB, excluding internal holdout, testCams, blurred, lossCams, or other excluded cameras, and bind the selected color/confidence to metadata. No external benchmark heldout/reference PNG images were read here. Neither modal color nor low texture certifies far depth or a semantic background; a foreground surface can meet appearance tests. Heterogeneous surroundings, view-dependent light, real holes/transparency, or object motion can still fail. Only a native paired probe can measure actual benefit/regressions, and diagnostic cache-derived results cannot establish strict acceptance.
