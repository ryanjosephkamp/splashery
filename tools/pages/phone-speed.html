Method and limits

The first view waits for the app’s first loaded-toy draw and a synchronous pixel readback. Idle and tap windows each last at least 2.5 seconds and include at least eight completed draw intervals. Rendering is forced during these windows; each readback waits for rendering. These are completed-draw intervals, including synchronization overhead. Idle animation callbacks are not counted as draws. Audio and automatic camera rotation are off. The toy’s own motion and tap action remain on.

A short tap may finish within its measurement window. Heap means the browser’s live JavaScript heap, which may include this results page, not total app or GPU memory. “Unavailable” means your browser does not expose that measurement. Transfer comes from Resource Timing and includes cache behavior; a phone run cannot force a cold cache. Compare before and after with the same browser, cache conditions, and temperature. The software-GL ranking is relative; these are your device’s own numbers.

Application errors are marked in the table. Browser resize-delivery notifications without an exception are retained separately in the JSON.
