These are tuning starting points, not measured material constants. Decay τ is an amplitude time constant; a ring takes about 6.9τ to fall by 60 dB. Mode gains are 1, .55, .30, .16, .09, and .05, multiplied by body gain.

How the taps are made

Each tap combines decaying, inharmonic modes with low-pass contact noise. Paper and fruit add a few short noise grains. Variation changes modal gains by ±12%, decay by ±8%, pitch by ±0.6%, level by ±8%, and grain timing by up to 3 ms. Switch variation off for an identical reference tap.

Each buffer has a smooth attack, mean subtraction, a 12 ms tail fade, and a peak of .5 before level variation. Playback uses one audio context, a 25 Hz high-pass, six sustained voices, and an 8 ms fade when replacing an older voice. Volume changes are smoothed. Very fast repeats are limited to one tap every 30 ms.
