The Playground on a phone: five proposals

The top five proposals from docs/audits/ux-phone-2026-10.md. On the left, a capture of main at 390 × 844 (headless Chromium, touch). On the right, the proposed screen, drawn in HTML. A dashed green outline marks what is new. Every control that exists today stays reachable in each proposal.

Proposal 1 · high value · small effort

The settings get the sheet

Code: styles.css (phone sheet rules), src/ui.js (setMode) Risk: low

While the settings are open, the shelf (search, categories, the toy strip) folds into one chip with the toy's name. Tapping the chip brings the shelf back.

  • The settings get about 410 pixels at 390 × 844 instead of 215. At 360 × 740 they get about 290 instead of 101.
  • The stage keeps its top 30%, so the toy still answers what you change.
  • The rule already exists for screens shorter than 650 pixels. This makes it the rule on every phone.
Proposal 2 · high value · small effort

Four buttons on top, the rest in a labeled menu

Code: index.html (the view buttons), styles.css, src/ui.js Risk: low to medium (many specs find these buttons by id)

The grid (the toys) on the left; sound, full screen and help on the right; "More" opens a menu of the rest with words and their state.

  • One row at 360 pixels, where today's eight buttons wrap into two rows.
  • Turntable, Tilt lock and Reset the view are icons people guess at today; the menu names them.
  • The four stay in view when the sheet is open, so sound and full screen are never hidden.
  • The credit line shortens to the toy's name and count; a tap shows the full credit.
Proposal 3 · high value · medium effort

A tool's options sit on the tool

Code: index.html (#tool-group), src/ui.js (tool switching), styles.css Risk: medium (the Tools tab's controls move, their ids stay)

Picking Paint, Clay or Magnet opens a slim tray above the tool row with that tool's few options: the colors, Brush and Clear for Paint; Add, Erase and Blob size for Clay; Strength and Radius for Magnet; Strength for Poke.

  • Today: pick Paint, then More, then Tools, then scroll, which covers half the toy.
  • The Tools tab keeps every option; the tray is a short way to the common ones.
  • Picking Orbit closes the tray. A swipe down on the tray hides it until the next tool change.
Proposal 4 · medium-high value · small effort

Full screen keeps a small control bar

Code: src/ui.js (setFocus), styles.css (body.focus), the hands bar Risk: low (after REC1's stage row lands)

Full screen keeps the tool row and REC1's stage row (Play, the hand, Reset and Record) on a dark, see-through bar. They fade after three seconds and come back with a tap on the screen's edge.

  • Today, full screen lets you orbit and tap, but not change tool, play a song or record.
  • Recording a clean take in full screen is the case REC1 just fixed; this makes it one tap.
Proposal 5 · high value in landscape · medium effort

A phone on its side gets a phone layout

Code: styles.css (the "short wide screen" block), src/ui.js (the narrow query) Risk: medium (a new layout to keep in the specs)

A phone on its side (short and touch) gets a full-height side sheet on the right, the top buttons in a column on the left, and the tool row under the toy.

  • Today it gets the desktop layout: the settings show about 120 pixels at a time, and the top buttons sit over the stage.
  • The side sheet opens and closes like the phone sheet; closed, the toy fills the screen.
  • Desktop and tablet layouts don't change.
