Splashery Sound Board

Every toy's tap sound, synthesized live in your browser from the same code the app uses (built from main on @@BUILT@@). Tap a toy to hear it. Toys that switch on and off have two sounds. Turn your phone's volume up; nothing plays until your first tap.

Sound review. Send your notes as a file, voice-typed is fine. Name only the toys you want to comment on; anything you don't mention stays as it is.

Toys that are changing get a Now and a New button, and Play through plays the toys in the current filter one after another, with the name shown large. Marks is optional, for tapping instead of dictating.
