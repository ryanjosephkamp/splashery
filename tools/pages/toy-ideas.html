Splashery Toy Ideas

Three new toy ideas arrive here every morning at about 7:45 (Eastern time), each with its tap effect and its sound. Mark each one Approve, Change (write what should be different) or Skip. Approved and changed ideas wait here until you ask the Operator to plan a lane for them.

What every idea follows
  • Kit-built from shapes, or a scan under CC0 or CC BY (the licence checked on its source page). Never BY-SA or NC.
  • No brands or logos, no firearms, no gore. Flags stay respectful.
  • Not already on the shelf or in the plan, and its tap and sound are its own.
  • The effect quality rules: real motion with solid pieces, separate things move separately, instruments are played, games follow real rules.

Loading the ideas…

"Change" keeps the idea with your note, and your note wins over its text; the Operator rewrites the idea to match on its next morning run. To build approved ideas, tell the Operator: "Plan a lane for the toy ideas I approved."
