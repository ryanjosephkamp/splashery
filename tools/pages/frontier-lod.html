Progressive spatial detail

A complete coarse sculpture arrives first. Spatial patches then replace their parents with finer points, prioritized by projected size, within a fixed point budget. Packed binary chunks are produced on demand in a local worker and transferred to the page.

      This is a local progressive decoding and LOD probe, not a network benchmark or a SOG, SPZ, or
      Spark decoder. It uses original isotropic splats, a small three-level hierarchy, CPU depth
      sorting, and Canvas 2D. It does not test learned LOD merging, million-point assets,
      anisotropic covariance, or WebGPU sorting.
    

    

      Memory counts current typed arrays, sprite pixels, and estimated canvas backing; it excludes
      process peak, object overhead, browser/compositor allocations, and delayed garbage collection.
      Times include worker startup and frame scheduling; raster times end at canvas submission.
