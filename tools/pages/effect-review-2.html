Splashery Effect Review, page 2

Clips of new and changed tap effects from every lane. Each clip starts a moment before the tap. Mark each one "Looks right", or tap "Needs work", write your note and tap "Send to the lane". "Just sharper" sends it straight to the lane as needing work, with the note that everything else looks right and only the sharpness needs work. A "Needs work" mark reaches the lane only when you send it with its note; until then the clip stays under "Not marked yet". With a note open, "Add a picture" attaches screenshots; they go to the lane with the note.

Page 1 filled its storage on September 30, 2026, so new clips come here. Clips from before then, and your marks on them, stay on page 1.

Loading the clips…

Clips are rendered in a headless browser, most of them at phone size, so colors and sharpness can look a little softer than on a real screen. Lanes add their clips here as they finish them; a clip that has been redone after your note is replaced by the new one. A note you have started but not sent stays in this browser until you send it or cancel it.
