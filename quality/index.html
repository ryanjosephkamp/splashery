Moving photo round 4: phone comparisons

These silent films visit every original file timestamp, or every retained JPEG-sheet sample frame, at the same camera. Video pictures are native. GIFs keep the app's profile frame budget and can hold a retained composite across original timestamps. These compare picture quality; separate live timing ledgers measure playback speed. Main receives the exact chosen native video picture for this geometry comparison. Long films use the baseline's actual local-file path only when absent from its shelf; each card records its path.
