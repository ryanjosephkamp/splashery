Splashery bake-off, blind review

Several coders did the same tasks, each working alone from the same instructions. Each card is one entry, named by a code, so you can't tell who made it. The judges already ran the tests and checks (their findings are under each clip). Your part is what tests can't judge: how it looks and moves, and whether it reads as right to a person.

How to mark: tapping the tags that fit is enough (several at once is fine, and they save straight away). A note is welcome too. "Looks right" and "Needs work" are optional ("Needs work" asks for a short note). Each task has a short "What to look for" box. You don't need to be an expert: a plain "this looks off" is useful.

Fair comparison: every entry in a task was recorded the same way (same renderer, same phone size, same moment), so clips can look softer than on your screen, but equally so. Compare entries with each other, not with the live site. The models stay hidden until you've marked every card. Nothing here touches the real site.
