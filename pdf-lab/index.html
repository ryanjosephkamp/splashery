Can a toy move in a PDF?

A little. A PDF can carry a recording of a toy with play and step buttons, and some PDF apps play it. The toy itself, the 3D one you turn and tap, can't run inside a PDF: it needs a web browser. So every Splashery PDF also carries a still picture and a link and QR code that open the live toy.

The short answer

Works everywhere

A sharp still of the toy, what it is and how to play, its credits, and a link and QR code to the live toy. Every PDF app shows it, and it prints.

Moves in some apps

A flip book: one tap recorded as pictures, played by the PDF's own script with play and step buttons. It played in desktop Firefox. Adobe Acrobat on a computer is documented to play it too; it hasn't been tried yet.

Out of reach

The live 3D toy. A PDF's script can show and hide pictures and fill in boxes, but it has no 3D drawing, so the toy's engine can't run there. Turning it or tapping it for a new result needs the browser.

Make one from any toy

With labs on, the Share tab has Save PDF. Pick Still + live toy (the default) for a page that works anywhere, or Moving recording (desktop Firefox and Acrobat) to add a second page that plays one tap. The dialog shows the most the recording can add before you save.

Two made that way, from the grapes:

  • grapes-still.pdf (80 KB): the still, the link and the QR code.
  • grapes-moving.pdf (627 KB): the same page, then a recording of one tap (36 pictures). Open it in desktop Firefox and press play on page 2.

Seven ways a PDF can move or respond

On October 4, 2026, Codex built eight sample PDFs from the real grapes toy and opened them in the apps on a Mac. Each sample tries one way. Download them and try them in your own apps.

  1. Flip book (W): 40 color pictures with play, pause and step buttons, played by the PDF's script. The method of the animate LaTeX package. 01-animate-widget.pdf (534 KB)
  2. Flip book with layers (O): the same pictures as layers switched on and off. 01-animate-ocg.pdf (544 KB)
  3. Text-box animation (F): 64 rows of letters redrawn by the script, in gray, like the Doom and Tetris PDFs. Click the empty box under the instructions and type n, then p. 02-pdfium-fields.pdf (510 KB)
  4. 3D points (D): 2,382 colored points of the grapes as a real 3D model for Acrobat's 3D viewer. Only points: no splat look and no tap. 03-point-cloud.pdf (2.4 MB)
  5. Embedded video (V): a five-second video of the tap inside the PDF, with the video also attached as a file. 04-embedded-video.pdf (153 KB)
  6. Still, link and QR code (L): the plain page; the link opens the live toy. 05-still-link-qr.pdf (42 KB)
  7. Attached web page (H): a small web page inside the PDF that links to the toy. Save it from the attachments panel and open it in a browser. 06-html-attachment.pdf (18 KB)
  8. Page flip (P): ten pages, one picture each, timed for a slide show. 07-page-flip.pdf (180 KB)

What to try on your devices

Download each PDF first (don't judge a preview in mail or on GitHub). For each app, write down its name and version, the PDF, and what happened.

  1. Mac or Windows, Adobe Acrobat Reader (free): open grapes-moving.pdf and 01-animate-widget.pdf. Go to the recording, press play. Does a grape fall? Try step forward and back. If Acrobat asks to trust the document, allow it for this document only.
  2. Same app, 3D and video: open 03-point-cloud.pdf, click the picture and drag (does a 3D model turn?), then 04-embedded-video.pdf, click the picture (does it play?).
  3. Desktop Firefox: drag grapes-moving.pdf into a window and press play on page 2. This is the one that should work.
  4. Chrome or Edge: open 02-pdfium-fields.pdf, click the empty box and type n then p. Then open grapes-still.pdf and click the link.
  5. iPhone: save grapes-moving.pdf to Files and open it (expect a still first page). Then scan the QR code on a printed or on-screen grapes-still.pdf with the Camera: does the grapes toy open, set up as in the picture?
  6. If you have them: Foxit Reader, PDF-XChange, Okular, SumatraPDF and Acrobat on a phone, with the same steps.
