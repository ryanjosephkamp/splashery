## What breaks rows 4, 5 and 6

#306's embed guide and link maker (`site/assets/hubs.js`, built from `tools/site-hubs.mjs`) read the toy list from `site/search-index.json` and take each toy's id and scene payload from the `#s=` part of each entry's `u` link (`e.u.split("#s=")[1]`, hubs.js line 23). On main every toy entry looks like `"u": "../#s=j.eyJhcHAi..."`: 397 of 420 entries carry `#s=`. After #307 (toy pages) the toy entries link to their own pages, such as `"u": "toys/cactus/"`, and in the merged build 0 of 443 entries carry `#s=`. The list is empty, `toys[0]` is undefined, and `updateLink` throws `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'payload')` at hubs.js:98. The page error fails site.spec.mjs:69 and spg.spec.mjs:28, and the empty toy id fails spg.spec.mjs:138.

Fix is on the #306 or #307 side, not in the merge: either #306's hubs read the scene from somewhere other than `u` (for example from the toy page, or a payload field in the index), or #307 keeps a `#s=` link in the index. Not tried here.
